ABOUT US

Phi Chi Theta is a co-ed professional business fraternity that brings together students who want to grow both personally and professionally. At the Iota Chapter, members build genuine friendships while gaining access to mentorship, leadership opportunities, networking, and career support.

Joining PCT means becoming part of a community that will celebrate your wins, help you through recruiting and college life, and introduce you to people you may never have met otherwise. Whether you're looking to grow your confidence, meet ambitious friends, or prepare for your future career, PCT gives you a place to do all three.
